A small one with a single job: the timeline stays at the bottom again. 📌

## 🎞️ Auto-scroll that actually follows

Since 1.4.0 the timeline's auto-scroll would jump to the bottom when you switched it on and then… stay there while new cards piled up below the fold. 😬 You'd see the toggle lit up red and still have to scroll by hand.

The pinning relied on SwiftUI keeping the bottom anchored as content grows, and it turns out that quietly stops working the moment the scroll offset is nudged off the anchor by anything at all, including the window's own toolbar inset. Chasing the bottom edge after every new card doesn't work either, because the cards spring in over a third of a second and the "bottom" keeps moving while you scroll to it.

So the timeline now does what the very first version did: on every reveal it scrolls to the pulsing "incoming" stub with the same spring the cards use, which means the scroll and the cards move together. 🎯 The red pulse stays glued to the bottom and the timeline fills upwards; switching auto-scroll back on after reading something snaps you down and keeps you there. Same CPU as before, this isn't the expensive scroll from 1.3.0.

## 🧪 Under the hood

- The `--replay` harness accepted `--rate 4` and then ran at 20 events/s anyway. Low rates work now, which is how this fix was tested at a realistic pace.
