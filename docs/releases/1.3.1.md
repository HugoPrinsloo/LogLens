A bug fix release for the two things that went wrong in 1.3.0. 🩹

## 💥 The crash

Stopping a recording while requests were still in flight could take the whole app down. The proxy kept a weak link to its server object and used it after Stop had already thrown that object away. With one Record button starting and stopping everything together, that happened far more often than before. Fixed, and soak tested with Record/Stop flipping every 5 seconds under traffic. ✅

## 🐌 The slowness

Three separate things were piling up on the main thread. 😅

- The Export menu was quietly re-encoding every single event to JSON on each screen refresh, and the new inspector wiring made the screen refresh on every batch. That alone was most of the "hammering". ⛔
- Pressing Record froze the UI for a couple of seconds while the Mac's proxy settings were changed. That now happens in the background, and the four network services are configured side by side instead of one after the other. ⚡
- Every new card in the timeline made SwiftUI lay out all 200 visible cards again, JSON boxes included. Cards now keep their layout unless they actually changed, JSON colouring is cached, and the timeline pins itself to the bottom without a full relayout. 🎞️

The result: events show up in the timeline as they arrive again, and the app stays responsive while a launch burst of ~300 events/s comes in. 🚀

## 📥 Install

Download the dmg, drag LogLens to Applications. Signed and notarized. ☑️ If you're on 1.3.0, LogLens ▸ Check for Updates… will find this one.
